The Heats

Each heat: your best two waves against a rival's. ★ win · ★★ win by the margin · ★★★ that plus the named move, clean (no wipeouts, no interference). Stars make your rank.

How to surf a heat

1 · Read the set. Lines on the horizon are the waves coming. Thicker lines are bigger waves. Bigger waves score more, so let a small one go by.

2 · Paddle in. When a wave reaches you, the ring around you turns green. Press Space (or tap PUMP) then.

3 · Ride. ↑↓ or the mouse steer you up and down the face. Going down gives you speed, climbing slows you. Space pumps in the middle of the face, snaps at the top (when you are fast), and cuts back to the curl when you are far ahead of it.

4 · The barrel. When Shazza calls "it's throwing!", the lip pitches over by the curl. Slow down (climb a little), sit low-to-middle right beside the curl, and you are inside. Stay high and the lip lands on you.

5 · The judges. Five judges score each wave out of 10. The highest and lowest are dropped. Speed, power (snaps, cutbacks, bottom turns), flow, variety and barrels count. Your heat is your best two waves.

6 · The moves. Floater (from heat 2): Space at the top right by the curl rides you over the breaking lip; steer down to land. Aerial (from heat 7): a fast bottom turn (the speed bar gold) straight up into the lip launches you; tap Space as you come down to stomp it. Deep barrel (from heat 11): sit right by the curl inside the tube. Moves inside 2.4 s of each other make a combo the judges love.

7 · A real heat (from heat 6). Rivals sit in the lineup with you. The priority disc says who goes first: whoever has it takes any wave they want; catch a wave a rival with priority is riding and it is interference (your second-best wave is halved). Catching a wave sends you to the back. From heat 8 the rivals read your habits. From heat 11 some waves close out: float over the section or stay high and kick out.

Stars. ★ win the heat · ★★ win by the margin on the card · ★★★ that plus the named move, no wipeouts. Stars make your rank: Grommet → Clubbie → Charger → Bells Legend.

A wipeout ends the wave. A wipeout means the heat is not flawless.
